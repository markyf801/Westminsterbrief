How this is measured

Calculated by Westminster Brief from Parliament's Written Questions API, through our own archive of written questions. Withdrawn questions are not counted. Only a full (substantive) answer counts: a holding answer, saying a full reply will follow, does not. Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays in England and Wales; recesses are not excluded.

Named-day questions

An MP can ask for an answer on a named day. A question counts as answered on time if it received a full answer on or before that day.

Ordinary questions

Parliament expects ordinary written questions to be answered within five working days of being tabled. We count a full answer as within the expected time if it is given no more than {{ ordinary_extra }} working days after the date Parliament lists the question for answer (usually two sitting days after tabling). Counting from that date rather than from tabling is what reproduces the Procedure Committee's published counts (see below).

Questions without a full answer

A question still without a full answer counts as not on time once its expected date has passed. Until then it is left out (see the notes above). Our archive keeps only each question's latest answer: where a holding answer was later replaced by a full one, the date of the full answer is used.

Median working days to answer

The middle value of the working days between tabling and the full answer, for all named-day and ordinary questions that have one.

Checked against the Procedure Committee's figures

The House of Commons Procedure Committee publishes departments' answer times for written questions. It uses {{ benchmark }}% as its benchmark. We applied the rules above to Parliament's data for questions tabled 17 July to 6 December 2024 and compared the results with the Committee's published counts for four departments. Ordinary questions matched to within about 1 percentage point (ours slightly higher each time); named-day questions matched exactly or to within 0.4 points for three departments, and 2.4 points for the fourth.
