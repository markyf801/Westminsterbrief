About Westminster Brief

Westminster Brief is an independent research tool that makes it quicker to find out what Parliament has said and done on any topic: debates, statements, Written Questions, bills and more, in one searchable place.

It is built for people who need to follow Parliament closely, including policy and public affairs teams, charities and trade bodies, journalists and researchers.

Who runs it

Westminster Brief is run by Mark Forde. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the UK Parliament, any government department or any political party.

Where the data comes from

  • Hansard: debates, statements and Westminster Hall proceedings from both Houses, from May 2025 onwards.
  • Written Questions and answers: from Parliament's Written Questions service.
  • Bills: from Parliament's Bills service.
  • Members: from Parliament's Members service.
  • Statistical publications: official statistics published on GOV.UK and by public bodies.
  • Stakeholder directory: public sources, including government transparency data on ministerial meetings.

Hansard is updated hourly while Parliament is sitting, and Written Questions each morning. Every item links back to its official source.

How AI is used

AI is used to tag debates and questions by topic and policy area, and to draft summaries and briefings from the parliamentary record. AI can make mistakes, so summaries are labelled and always link to the original source. Please check the official record before relying on anything for decisions or publication.
