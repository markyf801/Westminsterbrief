When a question in Parliament is contested, members vote in a division: MPs vote Aye or No, and peers vote Content or Not content. Each division records how every member who took part voted.

Many decisions are made without a vote, and a member not voting can have many reasons (for example being paired, unwell or away on parliamentary business), so absence is not shown here.
