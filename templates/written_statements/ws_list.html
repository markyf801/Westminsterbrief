Ministers use written statements to tell Parliament about new policies, decisions, reports and figures without making a speech in the chamber. Each statement is published on the day it is made.

A statement made in both Houses appears twice: once for the House of Commons and once for the House of Lords. Statements made out loud in the chamber are in the Hansard archive.
