Parliamentary committees

Who sits on each committee, what they are investigating, and where you can submit evidence · All-Party Parliamentary Groups

{% if not filtered %}

Select committees of MPs and peers scrutinise government departments and run inquiries into specific issues. When an inquiry opens, the committee usually publishes a call for evidence: anyone, including charities and individuals, can send a written submission before the deadline.

Bill committees and delegated legislation committees examine legislation line by line; they do not normally take public submissions.
