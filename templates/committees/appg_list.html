{% if not filtered and page == 1 %}

All-Party Parliamentary Groups (APPGs) are informal groups of MPs and peers who share an interest in a subject or a country. They are not official committees of Parliament and have no formal powers, but they can be a route for organisations to raise an issue with parliamentarians.

Many APPGs have a secretariat provided by a charity, trade body or consultancy. Each group’s officers, secretariat and registered benefits are listed below as published in the register.
