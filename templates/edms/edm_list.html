Early Day Motions (EDMs) are motions that MPs table to draw attention to an issue, event or campaign. Other MPs sign an EDM to show their support.

Very few EDMs are ever debated, but the list of signatures is a public record of which MPs back a cause. By convention, government ministers do not sign them.
